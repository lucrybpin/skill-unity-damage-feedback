# Asset provenance

- All bundled C# code, skill instructions, prefabs, demo scene and the original synthetic impact are distributed under the repository's MIT license.
- `SyntheticHit.wav` is generated from sine waves and deterministic noise by `scripts/generate_hit.py`. It contains no samples from third-party recordings.
- Text uses Unity's built-in `LegacyRuntime.ttf` and font material by reference. No Unity font binary is redistributed. Unity itself remains subject to Unity's terms.
- The source prototype's Game Over font and Freesound recording `394874__deleted_user_6479820__strong-kick.mp3` are **not included**. The public sound is a substitute, not the original First Wind Form recording.
- You can assign a different font/material pair or AudioClip in the Inspector. Check the license of any assets you add before redistributing your version.
